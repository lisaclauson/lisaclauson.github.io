About Me

I'm a Technical Product Leader with 16+ years of experience delivering complex optical and optomechanical systems across simulation and manufacturing environments. My work spans the full product lifecycle, from design and simulation through fabrication, hands-on alignment, supplier coordination, and large-scale software product delivery.

I specialize in bridging design, simulation, and manufacturing, translating complex optical and optomechanical engineering requirements into executable roadmaps and scalable product strategies. At Ansys and Zemax, I led product strategy for Zemax OpticStudio and OpticsBuilder, defining tolerancing methodologies used by engineers worldwide across consumer electronics, astronomical programs, aerospace & defense applications, autonomous vehicles, AR/VR systems, healthcare technology such as endoscopes, and real camera systems.

I bring hands-on optomechanical engineering roots from Panavision, a background in applied AI research for optical modeling, and a track record of cross-functional leadership across globally distributed teams. I thrive at the intersection of precision engineering, program execution, and applied technology.
